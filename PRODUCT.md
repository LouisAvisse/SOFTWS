# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Revenue and customer-facing teams (sales reps, managers, customer service, L&D)
and the enablement leaders who buy for them, largely at European enterprises
(EDF, LG, Sage, Groupama, Thermor and peers). They land on the marketing site to
judge, in seconds, whether Soft is a credible, modern way to train people for
high-stakes conversations. Context: evaluating, comparing, building internal
buy-in. They want proof of substance, not hype.

## Product Purpose

Soft turns static sales/communication training into adaptive, AI-powered
roleplay. Reps rehearse high-stakes conversations, get instant AI coaching, and
master a pitch before the first real call. The site's job is to make that feel
real, premium, and obviously better than slide decks and one-off workshops.

## Brand Personality

Composed, credible, quietly modern. Three words: calm, expert, human. The voice
is specific and plain, never buzzwordy. Confidence shown through restraint and
craft, not loudness. Speech and conversation are the core metaphor.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template feel: identical icon-heading-text card grids, tiny
  tracked uppercase eyebrows over every section, hero-metric clichés.
- AI-slop equalizer animations: uniform, mechanically repeating bars.
- Loud, gradient-soaked startup landing pages. Soft is calm, not shouty.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the conversation.** Voice/speech is the product metaphor; visuals
   should evoke a real person speaking, not abstract dashboards.
2. **Calm confidence.** Restraint, generous space, one decisive idea per fold.
3. **Craft is the proof.** Motion and detail must feel deliberate and real
   (a believable speech waveform, not a looped equalizer).
4. **One system.** All color/spacing/radius flows from the globals.css tokens;
   the brand blue (#4472ca) is the single accent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Every animation has a
`prefers-reduced-motion` fallback (static, no looping motion). Decorative
visuals (waveform, character) are `aria-hidden`; meaning lives in the text.
